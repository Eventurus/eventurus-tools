Chinese To-Go Cards

Essential phrases for taxis, dining, hotels and emergencies. Show the card or read aloud.

taxi
请带我去这个地址。
Qing dai wo qu zhe ge di zhi.
Please take me to this address. - Show the address on your phone
taxi
打表吗？
Da biao ma?
Do you use the meter? - Always ask before starting the ride
taxi
我要去机场。
Wo yao qu ji chang.
I need to go to the airport.
taxi
我要去火车站。
Wo yao qu huo che zhan.
I need to go to the train station.
taxi
在这里停就可以。
Zai zhe li ting jiu ke yi.
You can stop here.
taxi
多少钱？
Duo shao qian?
How much? - Check before paying
dining
我吃素，不要肉。
Wo chi su, bu yao rou.
I'm vegetarian, no meat please.
dining
我不吃辣。
Wo bu chi la.
I don't eat spicy food.
dining
我对花生过敏。
Wo dui hua sheng guo min.
I'm allergic to peanuts.
dining
有英文菜单吗？
You ying wen cai dan ma?
Do you have an English menu? - Many tourist-friendly restaurants do
dining
买单，谢谢。
Mai dan, xie xie.
Check, please.
dining
不要味精。
Bu yao wei jing.
No MSG, please.
dining
请问有什么推荐的菜？
Qing wen you shen me tui jian de cai?
What do you recommend?
dining
我要打包。
Wo yao da bao.
I'd like to take this to go.

我有预订。
Wo you yu ding.
I have a reservation.
hotel

Wo yao ban li ru zhu.
I'd like to check in.
hotel

Wo de fang jian hao shi ___.
My room number is ___.
hotel
有Wi-Fi吗？密码是多少？
You Wi-Fi ma? Mi ma shi duo shao?
Is there Wi-Fi? What's the password?
hotel
请帮我叫一辆出租车。
Qing bang wo jiao yi liang chu zu che.
Please call a taxi for me.
hotel
早餐几点开始？
Zao can ji dian kai shi?
What time does breakfast start?
hotel
我需要多一条毛巾。
Wo xu yao duo yi tiao mao jin.
I need an extra towel.

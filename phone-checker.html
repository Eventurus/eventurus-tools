Will My Phone Work in China?

Select your phone model and origin to check network compatibility.

✅
    Quick facts:
    • China's 4G uses Band 1 (2100), Band 3 (1800), Band 8 (900), Band 40 (2300), Band 41 (2600)
    • China's 5G uses n1, n41, n78, n79
    • Most iPhones and flagship Androids from outside China work fine
    • US carrier-locked phones (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile) are hit-or-miss — check here first
    • eSIM works on iPhone (mainland China models lack eSIM!) and some Androids

    📱 Best SIM options for travellers

    • eSIM (Airalo / Holafly): Buy before you go. Works on eSIM-compatible phones (non-China iPhone 14+, Pixel, Samsung flagships). Cheapest for short trips.
    • Airport SIM kiosks: China Mobile / China Unicom / China Telecom. Prices from ¥30–100 (7–30 days). Bring your passport.
    • Nomad / Ubigi: eSIM providers with China data plans. Good middle-ground option.

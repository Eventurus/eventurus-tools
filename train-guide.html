High-Speed Train Seat Guide

Everything you need to know about China's high-speed rail seat classes. Official diagrams & real cabin photos.

Good to know: All high-speed train seats face forward. Trains are very punctual. Boarding gates close 3-5 minutes before departure. Your foreign passport works at the自助 ticket machine at most major stations.

EVENTURUS tips for booking

  1. Book early. High-speed rail tickets go on sale 14 days ahead. For Golden Week or Chinese New Year, book the moment they release.
  2. Use Trip.com or the 12306 app in English. Both accept foreign passports. The 12306 app also issues e-tickets.
  3. Choose window seats (A or F) for the best views. On most routes, seat A = window, C = aisle on the 3-seat side.
  4. 1st class is the sweet spot for trips over 3 hours. More legroom, fewer seats per row, and only 1.7x the price of 2nd class.
  5. Business class is worth it for long routes like Shanghai–Beijing (4.5h) if you want to sleep or work in peace. Lie-flat seats on most G-trains.
  6. Bring snacks. Food carts come through but selection is limited. Pack instant noodles (the train has hot water) and a drink.

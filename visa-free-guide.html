China Visa-Free Entry Guide

China's visa-free policies have expanded rapidly. Find out if your nationality qualifies, how long you can stay, and which regions you can visit — all in one place.

As of early 2026, China offers visa-free access to nationals of over 75 countries across five different policy tracks. The hardest part is no longer getting in — it is knowing which set of rules applies to you.
📅 Last updated: May 2026. Policies change frequently — the most recent additions (UK, Canada, Sweden, Russia, Brazil, Argentina, several Middle Eastern nations) took effect between June 2025 and February 2026. Country lists below verified against the official National Immigration Administration (NIA) publication as of July 2026. Always cross-check with the nearest Chinese embassy or consulate before booking.

For travellers passing through China to a third country. Stay up to 10 days within designated regions.

55 countries • Stay up to 10 days (240 hours)

Hainan Visa-Free

30 days on China's tropical island province. No onward ticket needed.

🏝️
Hainan Visa-Free
59 countries • Stay up to 30 days • Confined to Hainan Province only
No onward ticket requirement • Direct flight to Hainan required
Can enter as individual traveller (not required to join a tour group)

🇭🇰 Hainan 144-hour from Hong Kong/Macau

Tour groups of 2+ people, organised by a travel agency registered in Hong Kong or Macau, can enter Hainan visa-free for up to 144 hours (6 days). Must enter and exit as a group. Covers citizens of any country with diplomatic relations with China.

Regional special policies

Niche visa-free tracks for specific regions and traveller groups.

🌿 Guilin 144-Hour — ASEAN tour groups

Tour groups of 2+ people from any of the 10 ASEAN countries (Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, Vietnam, Cambodia, Laos, Myanmar, Singapore, Brunei, Philippines) can enter Guilin visa-free for up to 6 days. Must enter/exit through Guilin airport and be organised by a registered Guilin travel agency.

🌴 Xishuangbanna 144-Hour — ASEAN tour groups

Same concept, different jungle. Tour groups of 2+ from ASEAN nations can visit Xishuangbanna Dai Autonomous Prefecture (Yunnan) for up to 6 days. Entry ports: Xishuangbanna Gasa Airport, Mohan Railway Port or Mohan Highway Port.

🚢 Cruise ship visa-free (15 days, 13 ports)

Tour groups of 2+ entering China via international cruise ships can stay visa-free for up to 15 days. Covers 13 coastal ports: Tianjin, Dalian, Shanghai, Lianyungang, Wenzhou, Zhoushan, Xiamen, Qingdao, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Beihai, Haikou, Sanya. Includes Beijing in the permitted area.

🛄 24-hour transit — all countries

Citizens of any country can transit through China without a visa for up to 24 hours, as long as they hold valid international travel documents and confirmed onward tickets. You must stay within the city of entry. Useful for short layovers.

👥 Tour group visa-free — CIS countries

Citizens of Azerbaijan, Belarus, Russia, Georgia, Moldova and Turkmenistan can enter China visa-free as part of a tour group (minimum 5 people). Stay up to 30 days. No geographic restriction — can travel across mainland China.

👤 APEC Business Travel Card

Holders of the APEC Business Travel Card (ABTC) can enter China visa-free for up to 60 days per entry. Multiple entries allowed. 5-year validity. Note: US and Canadian citizens are not eligible for visa-free China entry via the ABTC card.

Where to Stay in Guangzhou

Six areas, from the CBD to the fairground, compared by what they cost and what they connect to.

Guangzhou is a working city that happens to be pleasant. It rewards a base near a metro interchange more than a view, and it punishes anyone who books during the fair without checking the calendar first.

At a glance

  • Six bases, from ¥350 in Yuexiu to ¥1,400 in Zhujiang New Town.
  • Airport to Tiyu Xilu: about 50 minutes on Line 3, ¥7 to ¥10.
  • First visit: Zhujiang New Town, with three lines under it.
  • Canton Fair: three windows a year, rates two to three times normal.
  • Guangzhou South to Hong Kong West Kowloon: about 48 minutes.
  • Cheapest window: midweek in January and after the fair in November.

Last updated 2 November 2026

• • •

What is the best area to stay in Guangzhou?

For a first visit, stay in Zhujiang New Town: three metro lines meet there, the riverfront is on foot, and the airport train reaches it without a change. Business travellers at the fair should be in Pazhou, families and old-city walkers in Yuexiu, and anyone with a 06:00 flight at the airport. Everywhere else is a variant on those four.

Pick by the trip, not by the map

  • First visit: Zhujiang New Town, the CBD east of the old city, where Line 3 and Line 5 meet and the riverfront is on foot.
  • Old city and food: Yuexiu, around Beijing Road and Gongyuanqian, where Lines 1 and 2 cross.
  • Shopping: Tianhe, with Tiyu Xilu as the busiest interchange in Guangzhou.
  • Quiet and architecture: Liwan, around Shamian Island and the Xiguan lanes.
  • Canton Fair: Pazhou on Line 8, walking distance from the Canton Fair Complex.
  • Early flight: The Baiyun airport area, 45 to 60 minutes from the centre.
Rule of thumb: Guangzhou is large and the metro is fast. Two changes cost 15 minutes; a bad location costs an hour a day.

Zhujiang New Town: the first-timer base

Our default for a first trip.

What the position buys you

  • Transport: Line 3 runs to the airport and to Guangzhou East; Line 5 crosses the old city; the APM loop links the riverfront sights.
  • On foot: the Opera House, the library, the museum and the river promenade face each other across two squares.
  • Rooms: the densest cluster of international hotels in the city, which keeps rates competitive outside the fair.
  • Evenings: the Canton Tower lights opposite, visible from most river-facing rooms.
Watch for: listings that say Zhujiang and mean a 20-minute walk inland. Check the street, not the district name.
• • •

Tianhe and Shipai: shopping and interchange

The busiest ground in the city, and the most convenient.

Buying and eating

  • Retail: Teemall and the Tianhe Road malls sit within a block of Tiyu Xilu, which is also where Line 1 meets Line 3.
  • Food: the densest restaurant map in Guangzhou, from Cantonese chains to Sichuan, with prices below the CBD's.
  • Shipai: the village behind the towers is cheaper and louder, and a reasonable choice for a long stay on a budget.
Be aware: Tiyu Xilu at 18:00 is one of the most crowded platform interchanges in China. If you are carrying luggage, time your arrival outside the peak.
• • •

Yuexiu and Beijing Road: the old city

Walkable, cheaper, and closer to how the city eats.

The old city

  • Streets: Beijing Road is a pedestrian spine over Song-dynasty paving, and the lanes off it hold the dim sum the city is known for.
  • Transport: Gongyuanqian is the crossing point of Lines 1 and 2, two stops from Guangzhou station and one change from the South station.
  • Rooms: older buildings and smaller rooms at 20 to 30 per cent below Zhujiang New Town, with more character per yuan.
For a two-night stop, this is the area that makes the evenings work without a taxi.
• • •

Liwan and Shamian: the quiet side

Colonial arcades, banyan trees, and no traffic.

The quiet side

  • Shamian Island: a sandbank of 19th-century consulates and banks, now pedestrian, with the river on both sides.
  • Xiguan: the arcaded lanes around Liwan, where the old Cantonese houses and the market streets survive intact.
  • Hotels: heritage buildings and small boutiques rather than towers, so room counts are low and weekends sell out.
Be aware: the evenings here are quiet. If you want bars and shops at the door, stay east.
• • •

Haizhu and Pazhou: the fair

One purpose, done properly.

For the Canton Fair

  • Position: the Canton Fair Complex has two metro stations on Line 8, Xingangdong and Pazhou, and the hotels along its north edge are a walk from the gates.
  • Prices: a room that costs ¥450 in July costs ¥1,500 to ¥4,000 in a fair window, and the cheap end sells out first.
  • Booking: two to three months ahead for the April session, three months for October. Pay the premium for a refundable rate; the fair calendar moves and cancellations are common.
  • Elsewhere: staying on Line 8 towards Changgang or Kecun adds 20 to 30 minutes and halves the rate.
• • •

Baiyun airport area: for the early flight only

Ten minutes from the terminal, an hour from the city.

When it makes sense

  • Flights before 08:00: the metro starts too late, and a taxi at 05:00 costs more than the room saving.
  • Long layovers: a bed and a shower for six hours is a better trade than a city hotel you will not see.
  • What you give up: dinner, the river and the old city. There is nothing here but the airport.
T2 is the long-haul terminal. Airport North serves it; Airport South serves T1. Check which one your flight uses before booking a hotel that advertises a shuttle.

Canton Fair: the dates that move the whole city

The fair runs in three phases each spring and autumn, and each phase has its own crowd and its own rates. The 140th session ran 15 to 19 October, 23 to 27 October and 31 October to 4 November 2026. The spring session keeps the same three-phase shape — 15 to 19 April, 23 to 27 April and 1 to 5 May — and the fair confirms the dates for each session on its own site rather than years ahead.

PhaseExhibitsWhere to sleep
Phase 1Manufacturing, electronics, vehiclesPazhou if booked early; Line 8 otherwise
Phase 2Household goods, gifts, building materialsThe same, with slightly softer rates
Phase 3Textiles, clothing, health and leisureThe same, and the busiest for hotel bookings

The two-day gaps between phases are the cheapest nights of the month, which is useful if your meetings straddle them.

Rates start climbing about a week before phase one and drop the day after phase three ends. Booking a refundable rate in January for April is the single move that saves the most money on this page.

Booking practicalities

Four things decide whether a Guangzhou stay runs smoothly: which station you arrive at, whether the property takes foreign passports, when the fair is, and how you plan to reach the airport.

Foreign passports at check-in

Refusing to receive foreign guests is not permitted, and no separate licence is required. You will still meet a small property that hesitates, usually because the front desk has not registered a foreign guest before. Confirm in writing before you pay, and keep the reply with your booking.

Arriving from Baiyun airport

Metro Line 3 north extension runs from Airport South and Airport North to Tiyu Xilu in about 50 minutes, ¥7 to ¥10 depending on where you change. First trains leave the airport around 06:00 and the last around 23:00, later from T2. A taxi is 45 to 60 minutes and ¥100 to ¥130 including tolls.

Arriving by train

Guangzhou South is the high-speed station, 25 to 35 minutes from Pazhou on Line 2 and Line 8, and about 40 minutes from Zhujiang New Town. Guangzhou East serves the older Shenzhen line and sits on Line 3. Guangzhou station, near Yuexiu, is the conventional one.

Onwards to Hong Kong, Shenzhen and Guilin

Guangzhou South to Hong Kong West Kowloon is about 48 minutes, with immigration done at West Kowloon. To Shenzhen North it is about 30 minutes. To Guilin about two and a half hours, and Yangshuo is a change or a through service away. Our train booking guide covers passports and seat classes.

When prices move

Fair weeks aside, rates peak around National Day in early October and Chinese New Year, and fall in the hot, wet weeks of June and July. Midweek in January is the cheapest window that is still comfortable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to the questions that come before booking.

What is the best area to stay in Guangzhou?
Zhujiang New Town for a first trip, because three metro lines meet there and the riverfront is on foot. Yuexiu for the old city and Beijing Road, Tianhe for shopping, Shamian for quiet, Pazhou for the Canton Fair and the airport area only for an early flight.
How do I get from Baiyun airport to the city?
Metro Line 3 north extension from Airport South or Airport North, about 50 minutes to Tiyu Xilu for roughly ¥7 to ¥10. First trains run from about 06:00 and the last from the airport around 23:00 to 23:30. A taxi is 45 to 60 minutes and ¥100 to ¥130 with tolls.
Where should I stay for the Canton Fair?
Pazhou, if you can book early, because the complex is a walk from the hotels on Haizhu's north shore. Otherwise stay on Line 8 and accept a 20 to 30 minute ride. Rates in the three fair windows run two to three times the normal rate.
When is the Canton Fair in 2027?
The 140th session, the autumn one, ran 15 to 19 October, 23 to 27 October and 31 October to 4 November 2026. The spring session follows the same three-phase pattern: 15 to 19 April, 23 to 27 April and 1 to 5 May. The fair confirms the dates of each session on its own site rather than years ahead.
How do I get from Guangzhou to Hong Kong or Shenzhen?
High-speed trains leave Guangzhou South for Hong Kong West Kowloon in about 48 minutes, and for Shenzhen North in about 30. Guangzhou East has slower services to Shenzhen's Luohu station. Bring your passport; the Hong Kong leg clears immigration at West Kowloon.
Do Guangzhou hotels accept foreign passports?
Refusing to receive foreign guests is not permitted, and no separate licence is needed. In practice a few small properties still hesitate, so confirm in writing before you pay and keep the reply with your booking.

The 200 dishes in this translator, written out

This page translates Chinese menu items into English, and the list below is the whole dictionary in plain text: 200 dishes across twelve categories, each with the characters to point at, the pinyin to say, and the English name. Read it before you travel, or search it at the table.

At a glance

  • 200 dishes, twelve categories, from dumplings to ingredients.
  • Every entry has characters, pinyin and an English name.
  • Pointing at the characters is faster than pronouncing them.
  • Meat dishes are grouped separately from vegetables and tofu.
  • Ingredient entries help you decode a dish you have not seen before.
  • Dietary tags appear in the search tool above, not in this list.

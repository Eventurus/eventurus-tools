Do I need a Chinese phone number to set up mobile payments?

No. Both WeChat Pay and Alipay allow registration with international phone numbers. Alipay even has a dedicated International mode. However, set up both apps before you leave home while you still have access to your home SMS for verification codes.

Do I need both WeChat Pay and Alipay?

Yes, having both is the safest bet. WeChat Pay and Alipay together cover over 90% of all mobile payments in China and some smaller merchants only accept one. Both now support foreign Visa, Mastercard, Amex, JCB, Discover and Diners Club cards without a Chinese bank account.

Almost nowhere. QR code payments via WeChat Pay and Alipay dominate. NFC payments from Apple Pay and Google Pay are not supported at the vast majority of merchants. Samsung Pay is similarly not accepted.

There are two methods. Method A: you scan the merchant's QR code, enter the amount, confirm with PIN. Method B: you show your payment QR code for the merchant to scan - no amount entry needed, faster at supermarkets and convenience stores.

Most international travel insurance policies cover medical treatment in China, but you should verify before travelling.

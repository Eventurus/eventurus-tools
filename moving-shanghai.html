Moving to Shanghai
Your first week

A practical sequence for the paperwork, payments, transport and daily essentials that matter when you first arrive.

Administrative information last verified 11 August 2026

Start with the legal clock. Then make the city usable. The exact route depends on whether you are moving for work, study, family or a shorter stay.

Start here

Choose the route that applies to you

This guide gives the order of work. It does not replace case-specific advice from your employer, university, the Exit-Entry Administration or the 12367 immigration service.

Work

Let your employer lead the permit sequence

Confirm your visa, work-permit and residence-permit route before travel. Some eligible M-visa holders in Shanghai may obtain a work permit after entry and then apply for a work-type residence permit; this is not the same as changing an M visa into a Z visa.

Study

Use your university checklist

Keep the admission notice and any JW201/JW202 documentation your institution requires. The university should tell you whether a health examination and residence permit are needed.

Joining family

Match documents to the family route

Confirm which relationship, invitation and sponsor documents apply. Do not assume the work or study checklist is interchangeable with a family route.

Shorter stay

Keep the essentials light

Accommodation registration, a usable payment method and transport setup still matter. A local bank account or health examination may not be relevant in your first week.

Your legal clock

Four deadlines to keep visible

24 hours

Register accommodation after arriving at a non-hotel address. Hotels normally register their guests.

15 days

Complete the post-entry work-permit step where applicable, coordinated by the employer.

30 days

Apply for a residence permit when your visa and purpose of stay require one.

After a move

Register the new accommodation within 24 hours; residence-permit address details may also need updating within 10 days.

These are not universal instructions.

Work, study and family cases differ. Confirm the route before travel and keep written instructions from the organisation handling your application.

Before departure

Prepare what is difficult to replace

Documents, access to your home number and a payment fallback matter more than packing every household item.

  • Confirm passport and visa validity A passport generally needs at least six months of remaining validity and blank visa pages. Longer or multiple-entry visas must fit within the passport's validity.
  • Ask for a route-specific document list Collect the employer, university or family documents you have been told to bring. Legalisation and translation requirements vary; do not rely on a generic list.
  • Keep your home SIM active You may need it for banking codes and account recovery. Check that your phone is unlocked before buying a mainland SIM.
  • Install the useful layer Set up Alipay, Weixin/WeChat, Amap and Pleco. Save your hotel or destination address in Chinese and take offline screenshots of booking details.
  • Prepare payments without one point of failure Bring an international card and a modest amount of renminbi cash. Link an eligible foreign card to Alipay or Weixin Pay before departure where possible.
  • Check health and medicine requirements Bring prescriptions and insurance details. If a health certificate is required for your route, Shanghai advises booking at least two weeks ahead.
  • Check voltage as well as plug shape China uses 220V. Many modern chargers are dual-voltage, but hair tools and other high-power appliances may not be.
First 24 hours

Make yourself reachable, legal and mobile

Do the minimum required to reach your address, register correctly and pay for ordinary purchases.

On arrival
EveryoneAirport

Choose the simplest route into the city

From Pudong Airport, Metro, taxi/ride-hailing and the Maglev are the main options. The Maglev reaches Longyang Road in about eight minutes and the single fare was ¥50 when last verified; it is not a direct ride to most hotels. Hongqiao Airport is already connected to the urban Metro network. Save the destination in Chinese before leaving the terminal.

Same day
UsefulNot a legal prerequisite

Get connected

A mainland SIM makes deliveries, local calls and account setup easier. Shanghai's international-service centres operate at specified terminals at Pudong and Hongqiao airports, but counters and opening arrangements can change. An eSIM or roaming plan is a useful bridge if your handset supports it.

Within 24h
Required for non-hotel stays

Complete accommodation registration

Hotels normally register guests. If you stay in a private home, rented flat or other non-hotel accommodation, register within 24 hours. Shanghai provides online self-declaration as well as police-station or community service-window routes. The guest, host or accommodation provider may handle the declaration.

A Chinese mobile number is useful but is not stated as a legal prerequisite in the official guidance. Keep the registration result: it may be requested later.

Read Shanghai's official accommodation-registration guide

Payment fallback

Do not make a Chinese bank account a Day 1 dependency. International cards, cash and eligible foreign cards linked to Alipay or Weixin Pay give you several ways to pay while you settle in.

Days 2-3

Follow the route, not a generic timetable

Your employer or university should lead the administrative sequence. Keep appointments flexible and allow time for different branches to request different documents.

Work, study or family

Confirm the next permit appointment

Check the deadline, appointment location and original documents. If you are applying for a residence permit, do not let the first-week errands obscure the usual 30-day post-entry deadline.

Only if applicable

Attend the health examination

A health certificate is generally relevant when you plan to live, work or study in Shanghai for a year or more, and for residence permits valid for more than one year. Requirements vary.

The official guidance says to book at least two weeks ahead, avoid food for five hours while water is allowed, and expect the certificate in about three working days. Bring the documents specified for your route.

Optional in the first week

Open a bank account if it will simplify your stay

Foreign residents can apply for a renminbi account with valid identification, but banks and branches may request additional proof for due diligence. Ask your employer or university for a branch familiar with international customers and set aside half a day. Do not assume English service, a fixed completion time or an on-the-spot card.

Days 3-7

Build a usable daily routine

Once the formal route is moving, set up transport, food, healthcare and - only when you are ready - longer-term housing.

Days 3-4

Transport, maps and deliveries

  • Use Amap's multilingual interface for walking, Metro and driving directions.
  • Shanghai Metro supports more than one payment route, including transport QR services and direct contactless international bank cards at supported gates.
  • Save your building, compound gate and hotel address in Chinese. Delivery riders usually need a reachable number and precise entrance instructions.
Days 3-5

Food, medicine and a reliable fallback

  • Find one nearby supermarket, pharmacy and hospital before you need them.
  • Keep prescription details and insurance contact information offline.
  • Test one food-delivery order while your hotel desk or host can help with the address.
  • For an emergency call police on 110, ambulance on 120, or fire services on 119. English service is not guaranteed.
Days 5-7

Approach housing as a contract, not a lifestyle purchase

  • Verify the owner's identity or the agent's authority to rent the property.
  • Read the rent, deposit, other fees, repairs, invoicing and early-termination clauses before paying.
  • Confirm how the tenancy contract will be filed and who will support address registration.
  • After moving in, redo accommodation registration within 24 hours. If you already hold a residence permit, confirm whether its address information must be updated within 10 days.
  • Utilities can commonly be paid through Alipay or Weixin Pay, with offline channels also available. Ask for the exact account numbers at handover.
Avoid fixed promises about the rental market.

Lease length, deposit, rent schedule and agency fee are contract terms, not universal Shanghai standards. Treat live listings and quoted prices as time-sensitive.

Your first weekend

One simple day is enough

The relocation guide does not need to become a city encyclopaedia. Pick one walk, one meal and one museum or park.

Morning

Walk the Bund

Go early, before the riverfront becomes busy. Save East Nanjing Road or your preferred starting point in Amap.

Lunch

Try a local dish nearby

Choose by location and current opening status rather than relying on a permanent shortlist of restaurants or prices.

Afternoon

Check today's museum access

Shanghai Museum operates at People's Square and the East Museum. Basic displays, special exhibitions and peak periods can have different booking rules.

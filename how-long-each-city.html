How Many Days in Each Chinese City: 10, 14 and 16 Days

Give Beijing and Shanghai four nights each. For a ten-day trip we suggest two cities, for fourteen days three, and for sixteen either four bases or three with a slower last week.

Two things to know before the splits

The main leg

Beijing to Shanghai: published journey times include 4 hours 18 minutes, second class about ¥553 to ¥694, with about 54 departures a day.

Tickets

Rail tickets release 15 days before departure on the official railway channel, so trains at the end of a long trip are not on sale when you start planning.

How many days should you give each city?

Our working rule is two nights minimum per city, and three if you want a full day that does not involve a station.

The rule

Two nights, or three for a day without a station.

Beyond that we add nights by interest rather than by rule.

What the rule misses

The second half of the rule is less obvious. A city two hours away is not necessarily a half-day addition.

Checkout, the ride to the station and boarding can take the morning.

How do the train times change the plan?

Tickets release 15 days ahead on the official railway channel. Holiday departures can sell out as they are released, which has two consequences for planning.

First

Plan the shape, not the train number.

On a trip longer than a fortnight, the trains at the end are not yet on sale when you are choosing the shape.

Second

Fares are bands, not fixed prices.

What you actually pay varies by train, season and time of day, so treat the figures here as bands and check your date on the official channel.

Since 26 May 2026. The published ceiling on the Beijing to Shanghai line rose 20 percent, from ¥662 to ¥794.

Where does this planning go wrong?

Three errors account for most of the bad itineraries we see. None of them is about the cities.

1

The common error is a three-city plan built on flight timings, then run on trains.

The fix: rail is measured city centre to city centre. Published journey times include 4 hours 18 minutes for Beijing to Shanghai. Add the time to reach the station and the time to leave it at each end.

2

Treating a station name as a place.

The fix: Guilin to Yangshuo is 22 minutes and ¥26 to ¥34, but Yangshuo station sits outside the county town, so you still need a transfer at the far end. Budget for it.

3

Putting the long leg in the middle.

The fix: a 10-hour rail day is easier to absorb between two four-night stays than between two two-night stays.

Frequently asked questions

During a 16-day stay, how many days would you give each of Shanghai, Hangzhou and Beijing?

Four in Beijing, four in Shanghai, and three in Hangzhou, leaving two travel days and three nights to distribute. Add the spare nights to whichever city you would regret rushing.

Is 10 days enough for Beijing and Shanghai?

Yes, at four nights each. It is not enough for both plus Xi'an, because the internal legs start consuming the days you came for.

When should I book the trains?

Fifteen days before departure, on the official railway channel. Set a reminder rather than relying on memory, and book the long leg first.

Can I see Suzhou and Hangzhou from one Shanghai hotel?

Yes. Both are under an hour from Shanghai, with frequent departures, so neither requires moving hotels.
